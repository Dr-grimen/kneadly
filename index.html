What it does

Sit at a desk all day, or run, or lift, or carry a toddler around, and something goes tight. A shoulder, a calf, the strip beside your spine. You knead at it with a thumb and it does nothing, because thumbs get tired long before a knot does.

The Kneadly Mini does not get tired. It hammers a padded head into the muscle a few thousand times a minute, and the muscle gives up. Two or three minutes on the spot and it lets go — the same thing a sports masseur does with an elbow, minus the appointment.

What you are getting:

  • Six speeds — one is gentle enough to use on a sore neck; six is, in the words of one buyer, «like a rotary hammer»
  • Four heads — ball for big muscles, bullet for a single knot, fork for either side of the spine or an Achilles, flat for everything else
  • A display that tells you the speed and the charge — no guessing whether it will last the session
  • USB-C — the same cable as your phone
  • It fits in a hand — and a gym bag, a glovebox, a carry-on

It is for shoulders, neck, upper and lower back, glutes, thighs, calves and feet. It is not for bone, the front of the neck, or anything that is injured rather than tight — see the FAQ.

How to use it

Two minutes on the spot

There is no technique. There is one rule: keep it moving, and keep it off bone.

Pick a head, start low

Ball for a shoulder or thigh, bullet for a single knot, fork for either side of the spine. Start on speed one or two — you can always go up.

Float it over the muscle

Let the gun do the pressing. Move slowly across the tight area in small passes, and pause for a few seconds wherever it bites.

Stop when it softens

Two or three minutes per spot is plenty. More is not better — it is just sore tomorrow. Drink some water afterwards; it genuinely helps.

Questions

Before you buy

The five we get asked most. The full list is here.

Is it strong enough to actually do anything?

Yes. The most common surprise in the reviews is how hard it hits for its size — one buyer compares speed six to a rotary hammer. If you have used a full-size gun, this is not as deep on the very top setting, but for shoulders, calves and backs it is more than most people want.

Where should I not use it?

Not on bone — spine, shoulder blade, kneecap, shin. Not on the front or sides of the neck. Not on a fresh injury, a bruise, or anywhere that hurts rather than aches. If you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a blood-clotting disorder or a pacemaker, ask your doctor first. It is a tool for tight muscles, not a treatment for anything.

How loud is it?

Quieter than a hairdryer, louder than an electric toothbrush. You can use it with the TV on. You would not use it next to a sleeping baby. We do not quote a decibel figure because the supplier does not publish a tested one.

How long does the battery last?

The display shows the charge, so you are never guessing. Buyers describe it as lasting a good number of sessions between charges. We would rather not invent an hours figure — it depends heavily on which speed you use.

Why does delivery take 8–16 days?

Because your order ships from our supplier directly to you instead of sitting in a warehouse we rent. That is the honest reason, and it is also why the price is $79.99 with shipping included. You get tracking as soon as the parcel moves.
